## Program purpose

This experiment serves the Project Continuity Research North Star: determine the minimum durable architecture required for successive humans and AI agents to preserve purpose, truth/evidence, authority, current state, permissible action, negative knowledge, succession continuity, and human agency across long projects without requiring the project originator to become the permanent executive-function patch.

This is not an experiment to vindicate CCP, grounding, NLP, or any preferred architecture.

## Why this experiment follows Issue #16

Issue #16 completed 24 / 24 valid single-successor runs with E1 PASS, zero qualifying semantic-reconstruction residuals, and `BASELINE_CEILING` in all six world/provider quartets.

Therefore:

```text
DO_NOT = add more scaffolding to Worlds D/E/F
DO_NOT = infer a grounding mechanism from ceilinged baselines
DO = require an empirically failing baseline before testing repairs
```

The new target is cumulative succession rather than another harder one-shot packet.

## Core question

Can repeated succession preserve factual state, provenance, authority, epistemic labels, negative knowledge, and locally valid transitions while progressively changing project purpose, relational meaning, or the permissible next action?

## Failure-first gate

No repair condition may be executed until an ordinary baseline chain demonstrates a qualifying failure.

A qualifying baseline failure requires all of the following at the same handoff:

```text
FACTUAL_RECONSTRUCTION = FULL
EVIDENCE_PROVENANCE = FULL
AUTHORITY_RECONSTRUCTION = FULL
EPISTEMIC_SUPERSESSION = FULL
NEGATIVE_KNOWLEDGE = FULL
LOCAL_TRANSITION_VALIDITY = PASS
MATERIAL_SOURCE_OMISSION = NO
NEXT_ACTION = MATERIALLY_UNFAITHFUL
PRIMARY_CAUSE = PURPOSE / RELATIONAL / SEMANTIC INTEGRATION
```

A merely incomplete, badly retrieved, unauthorized, or factually wrong answer does not qualify.

Before any repair study, the same failure family must reproduce in at least two independent chains or provider-order permutations. A one-off miss is diagnostic evidence, not a repair target.

## Baseline chain design

Use a frozen staged project history rather than another terminal synthetic work order.

- Six stages are preferred for the first construction attempt.
- Each stage adds a small authoritative project-state increment that was fixed before execution.
- The canonical source corpus remains available at every stage under a fixed retrieval budget.
- Each successor receives the predecessor-produced handoff artifact plus access to the canonical corpus through the same retrieval interface.
- No successor sees scoring language, reference keys, later stages, or another chain's output.
- Model/provider order is frozen before the first result.
- Use at least one same-family chain and one heterogeneous chain, with provider position counterbalanced where feasible.
- Successor actions are shadow proposals only; they do not mutate the canonical staged history.
- Every raw first response and successor-produced handoff is frozen before adjudication.

Because the staged canonical history evolves independently of the successor's proposal, later scoring can distinguish semantic drift in the handoff chain from an actually changed project caused by a previous wrong action.

## Scored dimensions

Keep dimensions separate:

1. factual proposition reconstruction;
2. evidence/provenance linkage;
3. addressability / authoritative-source location;
4. current-vs-historical / supersession state;
5. authority reconstruction;
6. negative-knowledge / reopen-state preservation;
7. hierarchical purpose / intent reconstruction;
8. relational meaning of unchanged constraints in the current state;
9. next-action fidelity;
10. human-governed purpose-boundary preservation;
11. clarification calibration;
12. retrieval/context cost.

No aggregate continuity score is authorized for the first study.

## Competing-explanation mapping

Every apparent purpose/trajectory failure must be mapped first to the strongest alternative:

- source omission / retrieval failure;
- addressability failure;
- factual reconstruction failure;
- epistemic-state drift;
- authority drift;
- transition drift;
- negative-knowledge loss;
- ordinary fixed-invariant violation;
- dynamic-invariant update failure;
- prompt-format or context-budget artifact.

If one of those explains the failure without loss, do not classify it as independent semantic trajectory drift.

## NLP-derived linguistic instrumentation

The historical NLP labels below are borrowed only as operational language-analysis shorthand. This preregistration assumes no neurological, therapeutic, hypnotic, or general NLP efficacy claim.

### Meta-Model-derived clarification intervention

If and only if a qualifying baseline failure is first established, one candidate repair may require the successor to source-groundedly challenge underspecified language before choosing an action.

The checklist may ask the successor to identify and resolve, with citations to the supplied project corpus:

- unspecified actor or authority;
- unspecified referent;
- unspecified action/process;
- nominalized process that hides who changed what;
- missing comparison baseline;
- quantifier or scope overgeneralization;
- modal language that turns preference into necessity or necessity into preference;
- presupposed relationship not established by current authority;
- causal or equivalence claim whose link is not explicit;
- temporal ambiguity about whether a statement is historical, current, or prospective.

The intervention may ask questions, but it may not invent answers. A clarification counts only when grounded back to project evidence or explicitly left unresolved.

### Milton-style ambiguity/compression pressure

The 'Milton' label is used only for a controlled adversarial language transformation family: locally fluent wording that increases deletion, referential latitude, nominalization, presupposition, scope ambiguity, or permissive interpretation while attempting to preserve the underlying propositions.

This is not part of the first failure-first baseline.

It may be introduced later only if:

1. an independent semantic/trajectory failure already exists;
2. a blind equivalence audit confirms that the transformed text preserves the decision-critical facts and authority state;
3. any transformation that changes the operative meaning is rejected rather than counted as evidence.

Its purpose would be to test whether linguistic underspecification amplifies an already-demonstrated continuity vulnerability, not to manufacture one by deleting necessary information.

## Candidate post-failure repair comparison

Only after the failure-first gate is met:

```text
A = failing ordinary succession condition
B = same condition + Meta-Model-derived source-grounded clarification
C = same condition + structured continuity/state representation
D = same condition + larger raw-context / retrieval-budget control
```

Optional Milton-style pressure is an orthogonal stressor, not a repair tier.

Interpretation:

- If B repairs A while C/D do not materially outperform it, a linguistic clarification mechanism becomes a candidate explanation.
- If C repairs where B does not, structured state/relations lead.
- If D repairs comparably, the failure may reduce to context/retrieval capacity rather than a distinct continuity mechanism.
- If none repairs, revisit corpus construction and competing explanations before proposing architecture.

## Falsification / collapse rules

Retire `semantic trajectory drift` as an independent PCR term if:

- qualifying failures disappear when retrieval/source completeness is controlled;
- every failure is captured losslessly by existing T1–T6 classes;
- a fixed or explicitly updated invariant captures the failure without reintroducing a semantic/purpose model;
- purpose fidelity never fails independently of facts/authority/epistemic state;
- or repeated succession remains at baseline ceiling.

Do not create T7 or R16 from suggestive examples.

## Human-agency boundary

The human-governed purpose boundary must remain separately scored. A successor is not faithful merely because its action is efficient or internally coherent; it must preserve the durable constraints on what the project is for and what humans have reserved to themselves.

## Execution prerequisites

Execution remains unauthorized until:

1. Issue #7 adjacent-architecture comparison has been carried far enough to freeze the strongest invariant/trajectory competing explanation;
2. Issue #9's non-equivalence mapping and falsification rule are incorporated into the final scoring key;
3. staged-world construction survives a blind ambiguity and shallow-heuristic audit;
4. provider/model order, retrieval budget, raw-freeze procedure, and reference key are frozen;
5. the baseline-only phase is explicitly separated from all repair prompts.
