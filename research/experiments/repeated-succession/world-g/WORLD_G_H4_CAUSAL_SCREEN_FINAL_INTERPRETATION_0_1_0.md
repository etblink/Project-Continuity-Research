## What this does not establish

This result does **not** show that Milton-style relational underspecification is harmless in long-horizon succession generally.

The experiment did not test cumulative handoff-to-handoff compression, bounded source retrieval, retrieval routing, or semantic degradation across multiple successors.

It also does not support a Meta-Model repair experiment. The preregistered repair gate required a replicated qualifying MILTON residual, and none occurred.

## Consequence for the Milton hypothesis

The narrow hypothesis

`A SINGLE RELATIONALLY COMPRESSED H4 HANDOFF IS SUFFICIENT TO CAUSE ACTION-FIDELITY FAILURE DESPITE FULL INLINE CANONICAL CORPUS ACCESS`

is not supported in the tested models/world.

The broader cumulative hypothesis remains open:

`REPEATED RELATIONAL COMPRESSION ACROSS SUCCESSION MAY ALTER TRAJECTORY WHEN SUCCESSORS RELY ON HANDOFF ORIENTATION UNDER BOUNDED SOURCE-RECONSTRUCTION RESOURCES.`

That broader hypothesis is experimentally distinct and was explicitly reserved by the preregistration.

## Next scientific move

Do not make H4 more ambiguous and do not invoke Meta repair.

The next discriminating experiment should move from one-shot handoff pressure to a **cumulative H1 -> H4 succession chain** with:
- source truth still available and identical across conditions;
- a bounded, identical retrieval budget rather than the full corpus automatically in prompt;
- predecessor-produced handoffs carried forward across generations;
- matched CLEAR and relationally compressed chains;
- raw-first-response and handoff freezing at every generation;
- scoring at every transition so the first divergence can be localized;
- a full-corpus rescue control to distinguish relational/orientation failure from inaccessible truth.

This directly tests the larger Project Continuity Research question: whether an orientation surface can drift while authoritative evidence still exists elsewhere in the durable project system.
