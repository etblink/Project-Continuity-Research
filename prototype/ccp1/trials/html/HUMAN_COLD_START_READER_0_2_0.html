What you are being asked to do

You are testing whether this project can explain itself to someone who was not involved in creating it. You do not need to know Git, programming, AI research, or the subject beforehand.

Your confusion is useful data. If you cannot find something, say so rather than guessing.

Before you begin

Please tell the facilitator anything you already knew about this project before today, including whether you have read its repository, design conversations, or summaries.

A rough timebox of about 30 minutes is suggested.

Your task

  1. In your own words, what problem is this project trying to solve?
  2. What phase is the project currently in?
  3. What has to happen before the next major phase may begin?
  4. Is the current CCP architecture already authorized to control a live project? Why or why not?
  5. Where would you look to distinguish the big-picture roadmap, exact current research result, machine verification, and historical research artifacts?
  6. Pick one completed adversarial attack and explain what failure it tested, what passed, and what the result still does not prove.
  7. If asked to continue the project without the inventor present, what would you do next?
  8. What did you find confusing, redundant, hard to locate, or easy to misinterpret?

Rules for the first attempt

  • This is read-only.
  • The facilitator may help with browser mechanics only.
  • If you have a substantive question, record it instead of asking the facilitator for the answer.
  • Your first answers should be preserved before anyone corrects or explains them.
