# INC-016 — Locally rational parallel research became the program center of gravity

- **Project:** Project Continuity Research / CPI-0
- **Approximate date / phase:** 2026-10-04 to 2026-10-05; CCP-1 Phase 4 with CPI-0 as a parallel research track
- **Evidence status:** R4 — multiply corroborated
- **Confidence:** High

## Reconstruction

### 1. Observed incident

A legitimate parallel research track, CPI-0, was opened after a NotebookLM synthesis highlighted recurring governance/interoperability structures across multiple projects.

The CPI seed correctly treated the NotebookLM material as hypothesis-generating rather than authoritative. CPI also preserved strong mutation, authority and evidence boundaries.

However, CPI progressively became the dominant workstream. It advanced through increasingly deep questions about:

- cross-project semantic projections;
- source completeness;
- owner authority;
- signed authority capsules;
- native trust bootstrap;
- Hive Active-authority verification;
- synthetic Hive authority-state provenance and finality claims.

During that expansion, the parent Project Continuity Research roadmap still showed Phase 4 / CCP-1 as active, with the unfamiliar-human cold-start trial and post-trial review still required before CCP-2.

The divergence was not one obviously unjustified step. Each local continuation exposed another technically legitimate prerequisite. The cumulative effect was that a parallel track outranked an unresolved parent-program gate without an explicit program-level phase decision.

The incident was detected when the project owner explicitly asked whether the work had drifted from the big picture.

### 2. Local model frame / working assumption

Observable behavior treated each newly exposed CPI prerequisite as the most responsible next problem to solve.

The working pattern was approximately:

```text
LEGITIMATE_PARALLEL_RESEARCH
→ LOCALLY_JUSTIFIED_NEXT_PREREQUISITE
→ LOCALLY_JUSTIFIED_NEXT_PREREQUISITE
→ ...
→ PARALLEL_TRACK_BECOMES_PROGRAM_CENTER_OF_GRAVITY
```

Epistemic rigor inside the track remained high, so local correctness made the program-level drift harder to notice.

### 3. Authoritative reality

The governing big-picture roadmap remained:

```text
PHASE 4 — CCP-1 ADVERSARIAL HARDENING = ACTIVE
HUMAN_COLD_START_TRIAL_1 = PREPARED / NOT_YET_RUN
CCP-2 = CONDITIONAL / NOT YET AUTHORIZED
```

The unresolved unfamiliar-human trial directly tested the North Star question: whether someone who did not invent the architecture could recover purpose, authority, evidence, current state, uncertainty and permissible next action without hidden coaching.

CPI-0 was a valid research component, but it did not replace the parent roadmap or authorize skipping the human gate.

The CPI seed itself framed federated continuity as research that would eventually feed the later multi-project read-only program rather than silently redefine the active phase.

### 4. Human intervention

The owner asked:

> Have we drifted from our big picture?

After reviewing the roadmap, research seed, CPI seed and live issue state, the project lead classified the condition as:

```text
BOUNDED_ORIENTATION_DRIFT
NORTH_STAR_FAILURE = NO
AUTHORITY_FAILURE = NO
EPISTEMIC_BOUNDARY_FAILURE = NO
COURSE_CORRECTION = WARRANTED
```

The owner agreed with the diagnosis and authorized correction.

The owner also asked whether the NotebookLM audio/transcript caused the drift. The adjudicated distinction was:

- NotebookLM was the **trigger/opportunity source**;
- it was not governing authority and did not itself issue the bad instruction;
- the primary failure mechanism was a **locally rational research cascade without periodic parent-program reorientation**.

### 5. Outcome

Issue #53 was opened to preserve the reorientation decision.

The already-frozen CPI Stage-9B independent audit was completed rather than abandoned mid-gate.

Stage 9B returned:

`REPAIR_REQUIRED`

with two S2 findings:

- the synthetic produced-only finality claim was false at the claimed scope;
- machine-readable output overclaimed native/authenticated provenance.

A bounded Stage-9C repair deliberately **narrowed the claim** instead of expanding into a fuller Hive finality engine.

A fresh Stage-9D independent audit then returned:

`PASS_WITH_NONMATERIAL_FINDINGS`

with zero S2/S3 findings and independently closed the two material Stage-9B findings at the narrowed synthetic scope.

The program decision after that closure is:

```text
CPI0 = PARKED
REAL_HISTORY_EXECUTOR = NOT AUTHORIZED
REAL_KEYCHAIN_CEREMONY = NOT AUTHORIZED
CCP1_HUMAN_COLD_START = CURRENT PARENT-PROGRAM GATE
CCP2 = NOT AUTHORIZED
```

## Interpretation

### 6. Candidate failure dimensions

`ORIENTATION_DRIFT`, `LOCAL_OPTIMIZATION`, `RESEARCH_CASCADE`, `HIERARCHY_LOSS`, `METACOGNITIVE_DRIFT`

These are provisional dimensions, not a new frozen taxonomy class.

### 7. Logos relevance

Moderate to strong.

The principal failure was not that the local CPI evidence was fabricated or that authority boundaries were ignored. Most local steps were well evidenced.

The representational failure was higher-order: the active work representation increasingly failed to preserve the **relative authority and priority of the parent program gate**.

Local truth remained mostly intact while global orientation became distorted.

### 8. Agape relevance

Strong.

The North Star existed to serve future humans and successors by reducing dependence on the inventor's ongoing executive intervention.

The unresolved unfamiliar-human trial tested that purpose more directly than continued cryptographic/federation infrastructure.

Allowing CPI to dominate therefore risked optimizing the research system away from the human problem it was created to solve.

### 9. Missing executive/metacognitive function

Periodic hierarchical reorientation:

- reconstruct the parent program;
- identify the currently unresolved advancement gate;
- ask how the active sub-track advances that gate;
- detect when a bounded side track has become the de facto program without explicit authorization.

The missing function was not raw technical competence. It was **priority hierarchy maintenance under successful local progress**.

### 10. Possible architectural implication

Substantial parallel workstreams should carry an explicit parent linkage such as:

```text
PARENT_PROGRAM
PARENT_PHASE
PARENT_UNRESOLVED_GATE
THIS_TRACK_ADVANCES
TRACK_SCOPE
TRACK_STOP_CONDITION
LAST_REORIENTATION_CHECK
```

A periodic reorientation checkpoint should be triggered by major milestones, not merely by failure.

The check should be able to return:

```text
CONTINUE
PARK
RETURN_TO_PARENT_GATE
ESCALATE_FOR_PROGRAM_REPLAN
```

without treating parking as failure.

### 11. Counterfactual detection question

> What unresolved parent-program gate does this work advance, and has this track accidentally become the program?

Secondary questions:

- If this track stopped today, what parent-program uncertainty would still block advancement?
- Is the next local prerequisite more important than the unresolved parent gate?
- Has stage numbering inside a sub-track begun to create a misleading sense of overall program maturity?

### 13. Open uncertainty

The exact point at which productive CPI continuation became program-level drift is not uniquely identifiable.

Possible boundaries include:

- the transition from semantic federation into authority infrastructure;
- the transition from signed authority into native trust bootstrap;
- the transition from a synthetic Hive adapter into authenticated chain-state provenance.

This incident therefore supports the need for **periodic reorientation checkpoints**, not a claim that one specific CPI stage was intrinsically inappropriate.

It also remains an open research question how frequently parent-gate checks should run without recreating the opposite failure mode already seen in INC-009: excessive orientation overhead that prevents action.
